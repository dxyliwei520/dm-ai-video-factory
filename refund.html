Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. General

Thank you for choosing DM Dream Maker ("we", "our", "the Site"). We provide AI video generation and AI chat services. All recharges are issued as credits, which are consumed by AI compute resources.

Due to the nature of digital services (compute costs are incurred immediately upon use), our refund policy is as follows.

2. Refundable Cases

  • Duplicate payment: If the same order was charged twice due to a system error, a full refund is available.
  • Payment succeeded but credits not received: If credits are not delivered within 24 hours, a full refund or credit issuance is available.
  • Service completely unavailable: If the service is completely unavailable for more than 7 days due to our fault, a refund for the unused portion is available.

3. Non-Refundable Cases

  • Credits already consumed: Credits used for video generation, chat, or other operations are non-refundable.
  • Subjective dissatisfaction: Refunds are not provided for dissatisfaction with generated results or aesthetic preferences.
  • Terms of Service violation: Refunds are not provided for accounts banned due to violation of our Terms.
  • More than 14 days: Orders older than 14 days from payment date are generally non-refundable.

4. Refund Process

  1. Submit a refund request to admin@cccccxai.com.
  2. Provide order ID, payment date, and reason for refund.
  3. We will review and respond within 3 business days.
  4. Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days to the original payment method.

5. Payment Channel

Payments on this Site are processed by Paddle. Refunds will follow Paddle's refund process and be returned to your original payment method.

6. Contact

For refund-related questions, please contact: admin@cccccxai.com
