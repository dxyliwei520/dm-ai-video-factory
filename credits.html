消费说明

最后更新：2026年10月 · 本站如何扣积分、如何退回、VIP 与积分的关系

1. 积分是什么

积分是本站唯一的计费单位。1 元 = 5,000 积分； 每一次生成任务统一消耗 1,000 积分，折合约 0.2 元一次。 具体价目见 定价与计费。

新用户完成邮箱验证后赠送 10,000 积分（≈ 10 次生成），可直接体验全部功能。

3. 扣费与退回规则

  • 扣费时机：点击生成、任务提交成功时扣除 1,000 积分。
  • 提交失败：积分不足、未登录、参数不合法等导致任务未成立时，不扣积分。
  • 生成失败 / 未能入队：系统会把已扣的积分自动退回到余额，无需联系客服。
  • 积分不过期，用多少扣多少，余额可在页面顶部「💰 积分」处随时查看。
  • 如需查询某一次的具体扣分记录，请提供订单号或大致时间，邮件联系 admin@cccccxai.com。
没产出的，不会白扣。只要任务没有真正进入生成流程或执行失败，积分都会退回； 真出现异常扣分，核实后手动补回。

4. 积分与 VIP 的关系（两套，互不抵扣）

  • 开通 VIP 不会让单次生成变免费 —— VIP 生成同样按 1,000 积分/次扣除。
  • VIP 买到的是：会员期限 + 大额赠送积分 + VIP 专属模型与独立工作流。
  • 赠送积分与充值积分同池通用，不区分来源，先扣哪个都一样。
  • VIP 有效期自管理员确认收款之时起算，到期自动失效， 不自动续费、不自动扣款。
  • 会员期内再次购买，剩余天数顺延累加，已购时长不会作废。

5. 充值方式与到账

当前为人工确认制：在「👑 VIP」页下单 → 得到订单号 → 按页面提示转账并在备注里填写订单号 → 提交后由管理员核对到账，确认即自动加积分 / 开 VIP。 待备案与商户号就绪后将上线自助在线支付，届时另行公告。

6. 退款与调价

  • 积分属虚拟商品，一经充值不支持退款；系统故障导致扣分但未产出内容的，核实后补回积分。
  • 价格与消耗标准可能随模型成本调整，调整前会在本页与网站公告条公示；已充值的积分不受调价影响。
  • 生成记录（提示词与产物）不永久保存，会定期自动清理，请及时下载需要留存的内容。
  • 如有计费疑问或需要发票，请邮件联系 admin@cccccxai.com。

How Credits Work

Last updated: October 2026 · How credits are charged, refunded, and how they relate to VIP

1. What credits are

Credits are the only billing unit on this site. 1 CNY = 5,000 credits, and every generation costs a flat 1,000 credits — about 0.2 CNY per generation. See Pricing & Billing for the full price list.

New users receive 10,000 credits (≈ 10 generations) after verifying their email.

3. Charging and refunds

  • When charged: 1,000 credits are deducted when the task is submitted successfully.
  • Failed submission: if the task never starts (insufficient credits, not logged in, invalid parameters), nothing is charged.
  • Failed or unqueued generation: the credits are refunded automatically — no need to contact support.
  • Credits never expire. Your balance is always visible next to「💰 积分 / credits」at the top of the page.
  • To look up a specific charge, email admin@cccccxai.com with your order number or the approximate time.
No output, no charge. If a task never really entered the generation pipeline or failed, the credits come back automatically; genuine billing errors are corrected manually after verification.

4. Credits vs. VIP (two separate things)

  • VIP does not make generations free — VIP generations are still charged 1,000 credits each.
  • What VIP gives you: membership time + a large credit bonus + VIP-exclusive models and workflows.
  • Bonus credits and purchased credits share one balance; the source makes no difference.
  • VIP validity starts when the admin confirms your payment and expires automatically — no auto-renewal, no automatic charging.
  • Buying again while still active extends your remaining days; nothing is lost.

5. Top-up and confirmation

Confirmation is currently manual: order on the「👑 VIP」page → you get an order number → transfer the amount and put that order number in the note → the admin verifies the payment and your credits / VIP are granted automatically. Self-service online payment will be enabled once registration and the merchant account are ready, and will be announced separately.

6. Refunds and price changes

  • Credits are a virtual product and are non-refundable once purchased. If a system fault charges you without producing content, the credits are returned after verification.
  • Prices and credit costs may change with model costs. Changes are posted on this page and in the site announcement bar; credits you already purchased are unaffected.
  • Generation records (prompts and outputs) are not stored permanently and are cleaned up periodically — download anything you want to keep.
  • For billing questions or invoices, email admin@cccccxai.com.
