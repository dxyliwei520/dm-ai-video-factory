1 元 = 5,000 积分；每一次生成（视频 / 图片 / 音乐 / 声音克隆 / 动作迁移）统一扣 1,000 积分，约 0.2 元一次。AI 对话与提示词反推免费。

3. VIP 会员怎么买、怎么算

  • 在「👑 VIP」页选择档位下单 → 得到订单号 → 转账时在备注里填写订单号 → 管理员确认到账后自动开 VIP 并发放积分。
  • 有效期自管理员确认收款之时起算；到期自动失效，不自动续费、不自动扣款。
  • 会员期内再次购买，剩余天数顺延累加，已购时长不会作废。
  • VIP 专属模型与独立工作流正在接入中，上线后仅 VIP 可用，且同样按次扣积分。

4. 收款方式

当前为人工确认制：下单后页面会提示「请联系管理员获取收款方式」， 按订单号转账并在备注里填写订单号即可。待备案与商户号就绪后上线自助在线支付，届时另行公告。

5. 退款与调价

  • 积分属于虚拟商品，一经充值不支持退款；如遇系统故障导致扣分但未产出内容，经核实后补回积分。
  • 价格与消耗标准可能随模型成本调整，调整前会在本页与公告条公示；已充值积分不受调价影响。
  • 如需发票或有任何计费疑问，请邮件联系 admin@cccccxai.com。

Pricing & Billing

Last updated: October 2026 · Credit pricing and VIP plans

1. Price at a glance

1 CNY = 5,000 credits. Every generation (video / image / music / voice cloning / motion transfer) costs a flat 1,000 credits — about 0.2 CNY per generation. AI chat and prompt reverse are free.

3. How VIP works

  • Choose a plan on the「👑 VIP」page → you get an order number → put it in the transfer note → once the admin confirms payment, VIP and credits are granted automatically.
  • Validity starts when the admin confirms payment and expires automatically — no auto-renewal, no automatic charging.
  • Buying again while still active extends your remaining days; nothing is lost.
  • VIP-exclusive models and separate workflows are being integrated; once live they are VIP-only and still charged per run.

4. Payment

Confirmation is currently manual: after ordering, the page shows "contact the admin for the payment method". Transfer the amount and include your order number in the note. Self-service online payment will be enabled once registration and the merchant account are ready, and will be announced separately.

5. Refunds and price changes

  • Credits are a virtual product and are non-refundable once purchased. If a system fault charges you without producing content, the credits are returned after verification.
  • Prices and credit costs may change with model costs. Changes are posted on this page and in the announcement bar; credits you already purchased are unaffected.
  • For invoices or any billing question, email admin@cccccxai.com.
